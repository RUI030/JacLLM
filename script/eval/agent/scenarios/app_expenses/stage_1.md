Build an expense tracker web app in the spirit of Actual Budget. A form has three fields in this order: Description, Amount (a number) and Category (a dropdown with Food, Transport, Rent), and an "Add" button. Below it a table lists the expenses, newest first, and under the table show "Total: $X.XX" and one line per category with that category's total. Expenses are saved on the server. Keep styling minimal. Validate in the browser that adding "Lunch" 12.50 Food and "Bus" 2.75 Transport shows "Total: $15.25".
