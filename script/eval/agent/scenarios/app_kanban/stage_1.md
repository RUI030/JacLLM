Build a kanban board web app like Trello or Planka. The board has three columns, "To do", "Doing" and "Done". It starts with these cards in To do: "Write spec", "Design logo", "Set up CI". Each card has a "Move right" button that moves it to the next column (no button in Done). Under the To do column there is a text box and an "Add card" button to add a new card. Cards and their columns are saved on the server, so they stay after a reload. Keep styling minimal. Validate in the browser that adding a card shows it in To do and that moving "Write spec" twice puts it in Done.
