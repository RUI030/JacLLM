Build a one-page temperature converter. The page shows the heading "Celsius to Fahrenheit", one text box, a button "Convert", and a result line. Before converting the line reads "Enter a temperature". After typing a number and clicking Convert it reads "N °C is M °F", where M is the Fahrenheit value rounded to a whole number. If the box does not hold a number the line reads "That is not a number". Nothing is saved on the server. Keep styling minimal. Validate in the browser that 20 gives "20 °C is 68 °F".
