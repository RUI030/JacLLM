Build a one-page reading list web app. The page shows the heading "Reading list", one text box, a button "Add book", and under them the books that were added, one row each, in the order they were added. When there are none it says "No books yet". Adding with an empty box does nothing, and the box is cleared after adding. Books are saved on the server, so they are still there after reloading the page. Keep styling minimal. Validate in the browser that adding "Dune" and "Emma" shows both and that they stay after a reload.
