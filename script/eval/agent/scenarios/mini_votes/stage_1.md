Build a one-page vote web app. The page shows the heading "Tea or coffee?", two buttons "Vote tea" and "Vote coffee", and two lines "Tea: N" and "Coffee: N", both starting at 0. Each click adds one vote to that side. The votes are saved on the server, so they are still there after reloading the page. Keep styling minimal. Validate in the browser that two votes for tea and one for coffee show "Tea: 2" and "Coffee: 1".
